Brand foundations

Open Frontier is a family of free, public creation platforms for people who build with AI - where anything you make ships in one push and anyone can learn from it.

Purpose

To make publishing what you build as free and open as reading it.

The tools to create apps, games, sites, and knowledge are now good enough that a single person with an AI agent can ship real things. What is still gated is distribution: hosting, domains, review, discovery. Open Frontier removes that gate. You describe what you want, it deploys, and it lives at a public URL the same day - free, and open for the next person to read, fork, and build on.

Positioning

Audience

Solo builders and small teams creating with AI - the person who has an idea and an agent, and wants it live without standing up infrastructure. They value shipping over configuring, and they are comfortable working in the open.

Not for: enterprises that need private, access-controlled, compliance-gated hosting. Those needs are real - they belong to the "Pro" siblings (ProAppStore, ProWebStore, ProAgentStore, ProDocStore), not to the free, public Frontier.

Category

An app / game / web / knowledge store - a publish-and-discover platform. It sits where a hosting provider, an app store, and a template gallery overlap, but unlike all three it assumes an AI agent is the one doing the building and a human is the one approving.

Differentiation

Everything published is public and forkable by default, and every artefact is authored to be read and maintained by an AI agent as much as by a person. The store is not just where work is hosted - it is a corpus the next builder's agent can learn from.

Personality

  • Open - the default is public, forkable, attributable. Is sharing the source and the reasoning; is not "open" as a marketing word bolted onto a closed product.
  • Direct - plain language, no ceremony, ship first. Is "push to main and it is live"; is not onboarding funnels, sales calls, or "request a demo."
  • AI-native - built assuming an agent is the primary author. Is machine-readable structure and one-command actions; is not an "AI feature" sprinkled onto a human-first tool.
  • Generous - the free tier is the real product, not bait. Is giving away hosting and discovery; is not a crippled trial that nags you toward a paywall.

Values and principles

  • Open by default - public and forkable unless there is a concrete reason otherwise. The trade-off we accept: we give up lock-in.
  • Ship beats configure - if a step can be automated away, it is. The trade-off: we hold strong opinions and offer fewer knobs.
  • Author for agents and humans at once - every artefact is structured to be read by a model and a person. The trade-off: more discipline up front (schemas, metadata) for machine-consumability later.
  • Community over gatekeeping - discovery is curated in the open, not sold. The trade-off: we forgo pay-to-rank revenue.

Naming

The pattern is Free/Pro + noun + Store: the tier, the thing you make, and "Store" for the publish-and-discover surface. Free tiers are public and open (FreeAppStore, FreeGameStore, FreeWebStore, FreeDocStore); Pro tiers are private and paid (ProAppStore, ProWebStore, ProAgentStore, ProDocStore). "Open Frontier" is the family name for all of them.

Tagline: Build with AI. Ship in the open.
